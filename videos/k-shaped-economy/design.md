# Design System

## Format
- Aspect: 9:16 portrait (1080×1920) — Douyin vertical video
- Duration: ~3 minutes
- Language: Chinese (Simplified)
- Style: Professional knowledge explainer, data-driven

## Visual Identity
- **Preset**: Blue Professional
- **Background**: #fdfae7 (warm cream)
- **Primary accent**: #1e2bfa (cobalt blue)
- **Text**: #111111 / #6b6b6b / #9a9a9a
- **Display font**: Space Grotesk (headings, numbers, chrome)
- **Body font**: Inter (reading text)
- **Cards**: 4% cobalt fill / 20% border / 10-14px radius, no shadows
- **Progress bar**: cobalt blue

## Visual Language
- Data visualizations with animated number counters
- K-curve diagram animation (split into up/down arms)
- Industry icons for contrast (factory vs AI chip)
- Timeline infographics for historical context
- Clean typographic layouts for key quotes
- Shot transitions: fade + slide
